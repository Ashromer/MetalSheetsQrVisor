Where industrial precision meets craftsmanship, envelopes with an identity of their own are born. We don't mass-produce profiles: we shape the building's skin, piece by piece, with full control over geometry, material and finish.

General concept

One language,
four decisions.

Choosing one option within each of these four decisions configures the façade.

System, form, material and finish: combining one option from each block produces a unique solution. We don't just manufacture profiles: we offer an architectural language with which the architect composes the envelope at will. And that is how this catalogue is structured.

01 · Decide the

System

How the skin is anchored and modulated: exposed fixing (the anchor becomes part of the aesthetic) or concealed (a continuous surface, no visible screws). The system also defines the freedom of modulation — wider, narrower or made-to-measure panels — and conditions the assembly and the reading of the surface.

02 · Choose the

Form

The geometry of the profile: corrugated, trapezoidal, flat or volumetric. It is the decision that most defines the character of the façade. Pick it from the Origami collection or design it to measure with Uniko.

03 · Select the

Material

The base metal: steel, aluminium, stainless, corten, zinc or copper. Each responds differently to climate, structural demands and the project budget.

04 · Define the

Finish

The surface: colour (RAL/NCS), gloss, texture and perforation. The same form changes completely with the finish — this is where the final identity is fine-tuned. And the sum of the four is your façade.

Exposed or concealed · the breakdown

Exposed screw · fixing rhythm on the façade

Expressive architecture

Exposed fixings

Solutions where technique becomes part of the aesthetic. Modulation and connection points contribute to the visual identity of the project. This is the ORIGAMI range.

Exposed profiles

Interlocking overlap · no visible fixing

Continuous architecture

Concealed fixings

Clean, homogeneous surfaces where geometry and light take centre stage. A continuous skin, with no visible anchors.

Each piece is anchored by its return folds to concealed clips or battens behind the skin, or by interlocking with the next one: the screw never appears on the visible face. Since the anchor does not depend on a visible module, the panel width is free — wider, narrower or made to measure — and the façade absorbs thermal movement without apparent deformation.

Concealed systems

Rollforming

04 · The language of forms

Origami™

Every fold has a purpose.

ORIGAMI™ brings together a collection of architectural profiles conceived from carefully studied geometries to give the envelope identity, depth and expression. Each series explores a unique relationship between light, shadow, scale and volume: a library of forms to turn surfaces into elements with a personality of their own.

Five families that cover the whole geometric spectrum. We don't aim to extend the list endlessly: these five resolve any façade. The real value lies in what comes next — customising each profile in material, finish, perforation, direction and combination (FLOW), or designing it to measure (UNIKO).

Full technical data (dimensions, types and properties) in the per-profile product sheets — separate documentation.

05 · Infinite combinations

Flow™

Harmony is born from combination.

FLOW™ is the conjunction of different profiles from the ORIGAMI collection on the same surface. The architect combines several geometries, materials and finishes at will to generate rhythm, texture and visual movement: composing the façade like a musical score. Every envelope becomes a unique composition, never repeated.

Profile mixVariable rhythmTransitions

Combination strategies · three methods for a singular façade

A

Mixing families

Two geometries can coexist on different planes of the building or on the same surface, playing with texture.

B

Rhythm transition

A profile can vary its depth along the façade, creating a gradient of shadow and movement.

C

Gradual perforation

The perforation density evolves across the surface: from solid to translucent skin, controlling the light.

Every FLOW™ composition is resolved with the same construction systems of the range, guaranteeing technical continuity across all planes of the envelope. By mixing widths, heights and directions, alternating exposed and concealed fixing, and playing with materials and finishes, a catalogue of profiles becomes infinite possible envelopes.

The process · from façade to solution

Several geometries, one façade.

The versatility of the system allows the profiles of the range — or a UNIKO™ profile — to be orchestrated in many ways within the same envelope: façades open to the client's exploration. A concrete example: five ORIGAMI geometries — Pyramid, Symmetric, Asymmetric, Escaler and Kaotico — alternated in widths and directions to build a rhythm of their own. Each façade is born from the sum of several pieces.

Combination strategies · design examples

One building, all the strategies.

The FLOW pavilion brings the three strategies together in a single envelope: louvres and ORIGAMI profiles in different widths and materials, a made-to-measure perforated central surface and a skin that changes with the light. From day to night, the same façade never reads the same.

Examples · made-to-measure projects

Two UNIKO™ developments created for specific projects. A single made-to-measure vertical-fin profile, resolved with the range's two fixing systems: the same language, two ways of building the skin.

Concealed fixing

A continuous skin, no visible screws

The anchoring disappears and the envelope is resolved as a single continuous surface: with no joints or screws breaking the plane, the skin of vertical fins reads taut and silent.

The same building, a different skin
The system sets the character

It's the same vertical-fin project as the exposed-fixing version, resolved with a different skin. By hiding the anchoring, the volume reads monolithic and serene: only the metal and the light set the rhythm, and the façade turns corners and cantilevers without a single interrupting joint.

Construction detail
Interlocking overlap

Each fin locks over the next with a fold that houses the anchoring inside. The panel width stays free —wider or narrower depending on the project— and the fixings never show on the face.

Exposed fixing

The rhythm of the fixing, on show

The construction is shown openly: the ordered, aligned screws draw a regular rhythm across the panel and give the skin of fins a technical, honest texture.

The same building, a different skin
The system sets the character

The same vertical-fin project, resolved with a different skin. Here the system doesn't hide: the grid of fixings becomes part of the façade's language and gives it a more graphic, direct character, where you can read how it's built.

Construction detail
Exposed fixing

The precisely aligned screws mark the modulation of the fins and resolve the junction in a direct, legible way. Assembly and maintenance are simplified, without giving up the order of the whole.
